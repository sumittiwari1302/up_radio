1. Our role

UP Radio is a directory and listener. It organises publicly-available radio station information for Uttar Pradesh and links to the stream URLs published by the broadcasters themselves — Akashvani / All India Radio, community and campus radio, and commercial FM networks.

We do not host, re-broadcast, or re-encode any audio. When you press play, your device connects directly to the broadcaster's stream server.
